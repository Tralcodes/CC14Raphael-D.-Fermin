Let me introduce myself (real quick).

Quick intro (reddit-style, but make it semi-formal):

I’ve been obsessed with tech since I was a kid — enough that I became an iPhone collector and eventually started doing smartphone repairs, from soldering to the annoying little hardware fixes that make you question reality.

Photography is the craft, though. I’ve spent 7+ years on photography, color grading, and editing (photo + video). I like pushing smartphone cameras way past what they *should* be capable of, and I’ve taken online courses to sharpen my color grading so the final look actually tells a story.

Right now I’m a BSIT (ERP) student, learning more about SAP, SQL, and the business side of tech (yes, even etiquette — character development). Long-term goal: work in IT, either as part of a strong team or eventually leading one. Collaboration + innovation is the plan.

Fun extras: top song is “Alone” by Marshmello, and my favorite movie is F1 (Joseph Kosinski).
